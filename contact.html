Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all our products. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, simply return the item in its original packaging for a full refund or exchange.
How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within the continental US. We also offer expedited 2-day and overnight shipping options at checkout. International shipping times vary by location.
Do your products come with a warranty?
Yes, all Nexura premium electronics include a comprehensive 2-year manufacturer's warranty covering hardware defects. Extended protection plans are also available for purchase.
Can I cancel or modify my order?
Orders can be modified or canceled within 1 hour of placement. Please use the "Manage Order" link in your confirmation email or contact our support team immediately.
